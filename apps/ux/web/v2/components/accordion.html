Net metering is a billing arrangement that allows solar panel owners to receive credit for excess electricity they generate and send back to the grid. When your solar panels produce more power than you need, the surplus is exported to the grid, and your electricity meter runs backwards, effectively crediting you for that power.
The PM Surya Ghar scheme provides subsidies for residential solar installations. For systems up to 2 kW, you receive ₹30,000 per kW. For systems between 2-3 kW, you receive ₹30,000 for first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for additional capacity. The subsidy is directly credited to your bank account after installation.
For a typical residential installation (3-10 kW), the process takes about 4-6 weeks from order confirmation. This includes site survey (1-2 days), material procurement (1-2 weeks), installation (2-3 days), and grid connection approval (2-3 weeks).
Solar panels require minimal maintenance. Regular cleaning every 3-4 months and annual inspections are recommended. Most systems come with 25-year panel warranty and 5-10 year inverter warranty.
