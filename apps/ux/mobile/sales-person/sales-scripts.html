Opening Statement

"Hello [Name], this is [Your Name] from OneOhm Solar. We help homeowners in [City] save up to 90% on their electricity bills with solar power. I noticed you have a [Property Type] - is reducing your energy costs something you'd be interested in?"

💰 Price Objection Scripts

Objection: "It's too expensive"

"I understand. But let me show you how solar actually SAVES you money. Your current bill is ₹[X]/month. That's ₹[Y] per year going to the utility company forever..."

Objection: "Can't afford it now"

"Great news - we have zero-down financing options. Your EMI will be LESS than your current electricity bill. You start saving from day one!"

⏰ Timing Objection Scripts

Objection: "Need time to think"

"Absolutely! What specific aspect would you like to think about - the technology, the savings, or the financing? I can help address that right now..."

🎯 Closing Scripts

Assumptive Close

"Great! So let me schedule our technical team for a free site survey. What works better for you - this Wednesday or Thursday?"

Alternative Close

"Would you prefer to start with a 5kW system or go directly for the 8kW system that covers 100% of your needs?"

💡 Sales Tips

  • • Always ask permission before pitching
  • • Listen 70%, talk 30%
  • • Use customer's name 3 times
  • • Focus on benefits, not features
  • • Always end with clear next step
