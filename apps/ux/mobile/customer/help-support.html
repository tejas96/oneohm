Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my project status?

You can track your project status in real-time from the Dashboard. Go to "Project Timeline" to see detailed progress including installation phases, milestones, and expected completion dates.

How do I make a payment?

Navigate to "Payment History" from your dashboard. Click on the pending payment to view details and choose from multiple payment options: Bank Transfer, UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Net Banking.

When will I receive subsidy?

Government subsidy processing typically takes 60-90 days after project commissioning. You can track your subsidy application status in the "Subsidy Tracker" section. We'll notify you at each stage of the approval process.

How do I raise a service request?

Go to "Service Request" from the main menu. Fill in the issue details, upload photos if needed, and submit. Our technical team will respond within 24 hours. You can track the status in the same section.

Can I monitor energy generation?

Yes! The "Energy Monitor" section shows real-time generation data, daily/monthly statistics, savings calculator, and environmental impact. Data is synced every 15 minutes from your inverter.

What warranty do I get?

Solar panels come with 25-year performance warranty. Inverters have 5-10 year warranty (depending on brand). Installation workmanship warranty is 5 years. All warranty documents are available in the "Documents" section.
