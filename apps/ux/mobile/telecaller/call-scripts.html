Initial Contact - Warm Lead

For leads who submitted inquiry

1

Opening

"Hello! Am I speaking with [Name]? This is [Your Name] from OneOhm Solar. I hope I'm not catching you at a bad time? I'm calling regarding the inquiry you submitted on our website about solar panels for your home."

2

Permission

"Do you have 2-3 minutes to discuss your solar requirements? I'd like to understand your needs better and see how we can help."

3

Qualifying Questions

  • ✓ What's your average monthly electricity bill?
  • ✓ Do you own the property or is it rented?
  • ✓ What type of roof do you have?
  • ✓ When are you looking to install solar?
  • ✓ What's your budget range?
4

Value Proposition

"Great! Based on your bill of ₹[X], I estimate you could save 80-90% on electricity with a [Y]kW system. Plus, you'll get government subsidy and it pays for itself in 3-4 years."

5

Next Step

"I'd like to schedule a free site survey for you. Our engineer will visit, check your roof, and give you an exact quote. When would be convenient - this week or next?"

Common Objections & Responses

💰 "It's too expensive"

Response: "I understand your concern. But think of it as an investment, not an expense. The system pays for itself in 3-4 years through savings. After that, it's 20+ years of free electricity! Plus, with our financing options, your EMI will be less than your current electricity bill."

💡 Tip: Emphasize ROI and financing options

⏰ "I need time to think"

Response: "Absolutely! This is an important decision. May I ask - what specific concerns do you want to think about? Is it the cost, technology, or something else? I can help address those right now."

💡 Tip: Uncover the real objection

🔍 "I want to compare quotes"

Response: "That's smart! When comparing, make sure you check: (1) Panel quality & warranty, (2) Installation experience, (3) After-sales service. We use tier-1 panels, have 500+ installations, and offer 25-year warranty. Can I at least give you our quote so you have a good benchmark?"

💡 Tip: Position as the standard to compare against

❓ "Does it really work in monsoon?"

Response: "Great question! Solar panels generate electricity from daylight, not just direct sunlight. Even on cloudy days, you get 10-25% generation. The yearly average is what matters, and in India we have 300+ sunny days. Pune gets excellent sunlight throughout the year!"

💡 Tip: Address technical concerns with confidence

📝 Quick Tips

  • • Always be enthusiastic and confident
  • • Listen more than you talk (70/30 rule)
  • • Use customer's name 2-3 times in conversation
  • • Focus on benefits, not just features
  • • Always end with a clear next action
